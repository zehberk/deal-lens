How ratings work

  • Price is compared with local KBB Fair Purchase Price, then national FPP or Fair Market Value when needed.
  • A complete rating requires a vehicle-history report unless the vehicle is new. Title, accident, odometer, mileage, service, and warranty evidence can improve or worsen the price-based rating.
  • Risk has an accelerating effect: small risks have limited impact, while moderate and high risks reduce Deal Score increasingly sharply.
  • Listings without a history report receive a price-only rating. Listings without a price or compatible KBB data are summarized by reason rather than displayed individually.
  • CARFAX warranty status is an estimate and should be confirmed with the manufacturer or dealer.
